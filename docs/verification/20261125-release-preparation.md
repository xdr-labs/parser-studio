## 3. CI steps after the approval gate

The job `web-quality` stops at the two owner approval tests, so its later steps never ran in CI on this branch,
and the `*.isolated.ts` specs of `playwright.roadmap-followup-20261020.config.ts` are in no browser shard. The steps
were replayed locally with their own commands and environment (`ps-takeover-scratch/fin/postgate.py`).

At cba1fbf 16 of the 17 replayed steps passed; the step "Original-source and mixed-profile downloads, reviewed
recovery and large-batch Chromium" failed 5 of its 14 tests (14 of 14 on main af7de35 and at 6e59c36; 1 failure at d2b0fce
and 0688094, 5 from 1e909af on):

| Tests | Cause | Fix in 2f85c6a |
|---|---|---|
| Syslog description followed by key values; both mixed generic profile journeys; profile format transition (4) | Regression of 1e909af. The one-more-pair check judged every white-space reading mode. A text prefix followed by two to four key=value pairs behind a BSD Syslog header is the profile reading `syslog_prefix_kv`; with five pairs or more the same line is the older space separated reading. So every `syslog_prefix_kv` reading was refused and the sample stopped with "parser rule needs review" although main reads it. | The check judges only the readings that keep the event as message text, which is the subject of the owner decision (keeping the message is not parsing it). The bare-token cases of 1e909af and d4e98cb are refused as before. Unit test `boundedAdoptionStableReading20261125.test.ts` (1 RED before). |
| Text recovery (1) | Stale since the unmapped-field decision of 2026-11-24: the recipe the spec expected to be refused captured a value without mapping it; such a value is now kept under its own name, so the fix passes. | Test only: the refused recipe reads the value without capturing it ("would drop 1 of 3 source value occurrences from the final parser output"). |

Limit that stays (same as main): a line of that shape with five pairs or more is read as space separated
key=value; its pairs keep their paths under `generic.kv.*`, the Syslog header tokens are then positional vendor
fields. No value is lost. The owner confirmed restoring the behaviour of main.
